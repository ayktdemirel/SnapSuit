## Frequently asked questions

### How does SnapSuit generate headshots?

You provide 3 to 10 reference selfies. SnapSuit sends them to an AI image model that produces new portraits of you in the outfit, lighting, and setting you chose — tailored suits, cashmere blazers, city streetscapes, modern boardrooms, architectural lofts, and more.

The results are AI-generated images, not photographs. They are designed to look like you, but they will not match your real appearance perfectly.

### How long does a photoshoot take?

Usually **30 to 60 seconds**.

Keep the app open while your photos are uploading. Once uploading finishes and processing begins, you can leave the app — we'll send you a push notification when your portraits are ready.

### What happens if a photoshoot fails?

Your credit is protected. If generation fails because of a network or server problem, the credit is returned to your balance automatically and you can retry from your Dashboard. If a credit is deducted and you never receive a usable image, email us and we'll restore it.

### How do credits work?

- **1 credit = 1 photoshoot**, with full choice of outfit, colour, expression, hairstyle, backdrop, and accessories.
- **New accounts start with 1 free credit**, so you can try SnapSuit before paying anything.
- Additional credits are available as In-App Purchases:
  - **Quick Look** — 5 credits
  - **Executive Pro** — 15 credits (most popular)
  - **Career Suite** — 35 credits (best value)

Prices are shown in your local currency in the app. Credits never expire while your account is open, and are forfeited if you delete your account.

### My purchase didn't add credits. What do I do?

Open the **Store** tab and tap **Restore Purchases**. This re-checks the App Store for any purchase that was paid for but not yet credited, and adds it to your balance.

If the credits still don't appear, email us with the date and amount of the purchase and we'll sort it out.

### How do I get a refund?

All purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds have to go through Apple:

1. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
2. Sign in with your Apple ID
3. Select **"Request a refund"** and choose the purchase

We have no ability to issue App Store refunds ourselves.

### How do I delete my photos or my account?

- **Remove a portrait from your gallery**: open it in your gallery and tap the delete icon. This removes it from the app.
- **Delete your account**: go to **Profile → Delete Account**. This removes your sign-in credentials and signs you out permanently.
- **Erase everything we hold**: email [support@snapsuit.app](mailto:support@snapsuit.app) from your account's email address. We will delete your account record, uploaded photos, and generated headshots within 30 days and confirm once it's done.

Full detail is in our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

### What happens to my selfies?

They're used for one thing: generating the portraits you asked for. They are stored in our cloud storage and sent to our AI processing provider to create your images.

We never sell or trade your photos, we don't use them to train AI models, and SnapSuit contains **no analytics, tracking, or advertising software** of any kind.

Your photos are kept until you ask us to erase them — see the question above.

### Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and stored on Google Cloud. Server rules prevent any other user from reading your photos or account details.

One thing to be aware of: your generated portraits are served from unlisted public links. The links aren't published or indexed anywhere, but anyone you share a link with could view that image.

### Why doesn't my headshot look exactly like me?

AI generation works best with varied, well-lit, close-up photos. For the best likeness:

- Use 5 or more photos rather than the minimum of 3
- Vary the background, clothing, and expression between shots
- Make sure your face is clearly visible and in focus
- Avoid sunglasses, hats, masks, heavy shadows, and group photos

### Which sign-in methods are supported?

LinkedIn, Google, and Sign in with Apple.
